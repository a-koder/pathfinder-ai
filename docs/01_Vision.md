# PathFinder AI — Vision

## Mission

Help every high school student find a career path that fits who they are — through conversation, not forms.

---

## Problem

High school students face major decisions about college and careers with very little structured support:

- Most students don't know what careers exist beyond the obvious (doctor, lawyer, engineer)
- School counselors are stretched thin — often 400+ students per counselor
- Career aptitude tests give generic results with no follow-through
- Students don't connect interests → majors → careers → colleges in a coherent way
- GPA reality often goes unaddressed until application season, when it's too late

---

## Vision Statement

PathFinder AI is a memory-enabled, chat-first AI counselor that meets students where they are — helping them explore careers, understand college majors, and build a realistic academic roadmap based on their actual profile.

---

## Core Beliefs

- Students think better through conversation than through forms or quizzes
- Personalization requires memory — one-shot interactions don't build trust or insight
- Career discovery should be expansive first, then narrowed with context
- GPA and academic profile matter — guidance should be honest and constructive
- The system should feel like a knowledgeable, encouraging mentor, not a search engine

---

## Target Users

**Primary**
- High school students (grades 9–12) who are unsure about careers or college majors
- High school students actively preparing college applications

**Secondary**
- Parents who want structured guidance for their child
- School counselors looking to scale personalized support

---

## Success Looks Like

- A student can have a 10-minute conversation and leave with 3 career directions they hadn't considered
- The system accurately captures student interests, strengths, and GPA in natural conversation
- Returning students pick up where they left off without repeating themselves
- Recommendations are realistic relative to the student's academic profile
- A counselor or parent can see the student's roadmap summary

---

## Out of Scope (MVP)

- Scholarship matching engine
- Parent portal or counselor dashboard
- Real-time college admission APIs
- SAT/ACT score prediction
- College application tracking
- Resume or essay builder
